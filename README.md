# Simulation Internet Basketball Association (SIBA)

The Simulation Internet Basketball Association (SIBA) is a fictional basketball league created by my father and me as a way to bond over our love of basketball 🏀 and stats 👩🏾‍🔬. We have two leagues, a professional league and a college league. The pro league consists of 30 teams from around the US and Canada. Players manage their team's finances, trades, draft picks, and team strategies to make the playoffs and ultimately, win the coveted championship. In the college league, choose up to three of the 350+ Division I colleges and universities and recruit, train, and educate young men as you seek a spot in the main tournament or one of the sub-tournaments.

## Original Motivation

The simulation for the league is handled by the pro and college basketball games from [Wolverine Studios](https://www.wolverinestudios.com/). However, we still needed a place to host the rules, standings, and announcements. My dad enjoys computers and even dibbles with various programs or hardware from time to time. But, websites aren't his strong suit, while that is my direct wheelhouse. So after working with him on the design and basic structure of the website, I got to work.

Originally, I had decided to use this opportunity to learn more about React, serverless architecture, and graphic design. However, as the league and site grown, I've branched out into other technologies and techniques. I would consider this my forever-learning project. 😅
