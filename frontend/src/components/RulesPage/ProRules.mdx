## League Details

- **Salary Cap:** $200,000,000
- **Luxury Cap:** $550,000,000
- **Rating Scale**: 1-100 Points Range
- **Team Owners' Characteristics:** Randomized
- **Trade Restrictions:** All trades can be veto by the Commissioners and/or the Trade Committee.
- **Draft Restrictions:**
  - Players are allowed to enter into the SIBA from the SICBA (college league).
  - High school players **cannot** enter the SIBA directly.
  - Foreign players are allowed to enter the SIBA.
